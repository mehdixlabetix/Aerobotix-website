01

Who we are

A little introduction

Curiosity becomes capability.

The AeRobotiX club is a non-profit science club, born from the merger of INSAT's Mechatronics and CCE clubs.

AeRobotiX develops robotic and automated applications with value across robotics, home automation, aeronautics, automotive, and security. Its history is shaped by regular participation in national and international competitions.

Members receive strong technical training and an exceptional managerial experience. Through real projects, team collaboration, and contact with professionals, they turn academic knowledge into practical engineering skills.
